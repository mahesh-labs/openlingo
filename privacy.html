OpenLingo Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 27, 2026

← Back to OpenLingo

1. Local-First & Zero-Server Design

OpenLingo is a free, open-source language learning application. We do not operate any central user database, analytics tracker, or advertising network. By default, all learning progress, XP, streaks, character mastery, and spaced-repetition flashcard history are stored exclusively on your own device.

2. Optional Google Sign-In & Google Drive Sync

If you choose to click Continue with Google, OpenLingo requests only your basic profile information (openid, email, profile) to display your signed-in account and the app-specific https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope.

  • OpenLingo can only read and write its own hidden backup file (openlingo-backup.json) inside your personal Google Drive AppData folder.
  • OpenLingo cannot view, access, modify, or delete any other files in your Google Drive.
  • Data flows directly between your browser/app and Google's official APIs — never through any third-party OpenLingo server.

3. Your Control & Data Deletion

You can export your data as a portable JSON file, reset local progress, or sign out at any time from Backup & Restore. You may also revoke OpenLingo's Google Drive access at any time from your Google Account Permissions page.

4. Open Source & Contact

OpenLingo's full source code is publicly auditable at github.com/mahesh-labs/openlingo. For questions, open an issue on GitHub.
